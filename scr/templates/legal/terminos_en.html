These terms explain how Lüp works and what each person who uses it agrees to. By creating an account, on the website or in the app, you accept these terms and our Privacy Policy.

1. What Lüp is

Lüp is a platform from Alta Gracia, Córdoba (Argentina) that connects stores with surplus food to consumers who buy it at a discount and to social organizations that receive it as a donation.

Lüp does not sell or buy food. Each sale is an agreement between the store and the consumer, and each donation is between the store and the organization. Lüp does not charge for products either: consumers reserve for free and pay the store directly when they pick up.

2. Accounts

  • There are three account types: consumer, store and organization. The information you enter must be true.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password safe. Lüp will never ask for it by email, chat or WhatsApp.
  • People under 18 need permission from a responsible adult to use Lüp.
  • Lüp may suspend accounts that don't follow these terms.

3. Stores

  • Only food that is fit for consumption and complies with the Argentine Food Code may be posted. Posting expired, spoiled or damaged products is forbidden.
  • The pickup deadline cannot be later than the product's expiration date. Stores expressly declare this when posting.
  • The store is solely responsible for the quality of the food: expiration dates, storage, cold chain, labeling and information about ingredients and allergens.
  • The posted price is the final price the consumer pays, and stores must honor confirmed reservations.
  • Lüp may remove posts that don't follow these rules.

4. Consumers

  • Reserving is free. When you reserve, you get a pickup code to show at the store when you pay.
  • You must pick up during the store's opening hours and before the pickup deadline. If you don't pick up in time, the reservation may be cancelled.
  • You can cancel any reservation for free before picking it up, from "My dashboard".
  • If there's a problem with a product, the store that sold it is responsible. You can contact us and we'll help you reach them.

5. Donations

  • Donations are governed by Argentine Law 25,989 (Ley Donal) as amended by Law 27,454. They are always free: Lüp charges nothing for them.
  • Only food that is fit for consumption and not expired may be donated.
  • Organizations that receive donations may not sell them under any circumstances, and must distribute them among the people they assist.
  • Lüp keeps the date, the food details and the organization of each approved donation, so stores have a record of what they donated.

6. Lüp Points

  • Consumers earn 1 point for every $2,000 of a picked-up purchase. Only full blocks count, and points are added when the store confirms the pickup.
  • Points can be redeemed for the merch shown on the "Lüp Points" page. When you redeem, you get a code and the Lüp team contacts you to arrange delivery.
  • A redemption that hasn't been delivered yet can be cancelled, and the points go back to your account.
  • Points are not money, cannot be transferred to another person and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Lüp may change the merch catalog or the points required for each item, announcing it on the site.

7. Reviews

Only people who picked up a purchase can review it. Reviews must be truthful and respectful. Lüp may remove reviews with insults, other people's personal information or content unrelated to the purchase.

8. AI help assistant

The "Lü" help chat and the "Fill in with AI" feature use artificial intelligence and can make mistakes. Always review the information before posting. Don't share passwords or card details in the chat. If you need help from a person, use "Talk to a person".

9. Lüp's responsibility

Lüp commits to keeping the platform running and protecting its users' data, but is not responsible for the quality of the food, which depends on each store, or for agreements between users. The platform may have interruptions due to maintenance or technical problems.
