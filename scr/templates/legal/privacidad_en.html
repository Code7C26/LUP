This policy explains what personal data Lüp keeps, what it uses it for and how you can control it, in line with Argentine Law 25,326 on Personal Data Protection.

1. What data we keep

  • Your account details: name, username, email, phone, city and profile photo (if you upload one). Your password is stored encrypted: nobody can see it, not even the Lüp team.
  • Stores and organizations: name, address, CUIT tax ID, opening hours, logo and store location (if they add it).
  • Your activity on Lüp: reservations, pickups, donations, reviews, points and redemptions.
  • Help chat conversations: the messages you write and the page you wrote from.
  • Phone (app only): an identifier to send you notifications, if you allow them.

Your location is optional. If you share it to find nearby offers, it's used only for that search and stays in your browser: Lüp doesn't store it in its database.

2. What we use it for

  • To make reservations, pickups, donations and Lüp Points work.
  • To send you email notifications (reservations, pickup codes, reminders and replies). You can turn them off in "My details" or with the "Dejar de recibir avisos" (unsubscribe) link in each email.
  • To answer your questions in the help chat.
  • To show each store statistics about its own sales.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

3. External services we use

To work, Lüp uses services from other companies, some with servers outside Argentina:

  • Render: hosts the site, the database and the photos.
  • Gmail (Google): sends email notifications.
  • Google Maps: shows maps and directions to each store.
  • Anthropic (Claude): processes help chat messages and photos analyzed with "Fill in with AI" to generate responses. Along with your message, we send account details that help answer you, such as your pending reservations.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies only to keep you logged in, protect forms and remember your language. Your browser also stores your preferences, such as dark mode, your recent searches and the location you shared. We don't use advertising cookies.

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data. You can change most of it yourself in "My details". To delete your account or request a copy of your data, write to us from the help chat ("Talk to a person") or on WhatsApp at +54 9 3547 62-0629. We'll reply within the time limits set by Law 25,326.

The Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), as the supervisory authority under Law No. 25,326, is empowered to handle complaints and claims from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules.

6. How long we keep data

For as long as your account is active. If you delete it, we erase your personal data, except what the law requires us to keep, such as the record of donations.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll announce it on the site.
